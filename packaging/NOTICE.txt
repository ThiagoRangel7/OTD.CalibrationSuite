Calibration Suite development filter package

Includes CalibrationSuite.Filter.dll and CalibrationSuite.Core.dll built from
this repository, plus NetTopologySuite 2.6.0 (BSD-3-Clause).
NetTopologySuite's complete upstream licensing notice is included alongside.
Source: https://github.com/NetTopologySuite/NetTopologySuite/tree/v2.6.0

Requires OpenTabletDriver 0.6.7 and its .NET 8 runtime. Host-owned OTD assemblies
are not redistributed or replaced. No Calibrator/RPC dependencies are included.
This is a local development artifact, not a published release.
